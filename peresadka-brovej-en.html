About the Procedure

Eyebrow Transplant —
Natural Shape and Fullness

Eyebrows play a key role not only in expressing emotions but also serve a protective function by preventing dust and moisture from entering the eyes. They shape the harmonious features of the face and accentuate the gaze. However, not everyone is naturally blessed with thick eyebrows, and their growth may slow down for various reasons.

Even regular plucking or the aftermath of permanent makeup can damage hair follicles. Loss of eyebrow thickness can be caused by the following factors:

tattooing and micro-scar formation, skin diseases, burns or injuries, genetic predisposition.

After the procedure, eyebrows acquire an absolutely natural appearance. In the first 1–2 years, the hair may grow slightly faster; however, its growth stabilizes over time.

How It Works

Stages of Eyebrow Transplant

The entire procedure takes 2–4 hours and is performed under local anesthesia in one day. Stay in Istanbul — only 2 days.

1
Online Consultation and Candidate Assessment
First, we determine whether eyebrow transplantation is suitable for you. Health condition, eyebrow loss type, and shape preferences are evaluated. The consultation is free of charge.
2
Design Development and Shape Approval
The doctor thoroughly works out the shape of the eyebrows, taking into account eye shape, facial proportions, and patient preferences. Only after full approval of the sketch does the procedure begin.
3
Local Anesthesia
The eyebrow area and donor zone (occiput) are numbed with local anesthesia. The patient does not feel pain throughout the entire procedure.
4
Hair Follicle Extraction
Hair grafts (500–1000 units) are taken from the occipital area using the FUE or DHI method with a micromotor. The donor zone leaves no visible scars.
5
Graft Implantation
Each graft is implanted into the eyebrow area at a precise angle and in the correct direction. The DHI method allows maximum density and natural direction of growth.
6
Post-Operative Care and Instructions
After completion, the patient receives detailed care instructions. After 24 hours, the transplanted area can be gently washed. Within 3–4 months, the eyebrows take their final shape.

Questions About Eyebrow Transplant

Eyebrow transplantation is a procedure that can be performed by anyone who is not satisfied with the aesthetic appearance of their eyebrows and does not have chronic diseases that would prevent surgery, regardless of gender or age.

It is important that the procedure is carried out at a specialized clinic. We, Este in Turkey, perform eyebrow transplantation in sterile conditions with our team of specialists. If you would like to undergo eyebrow transplantation, you can contact us for detailed information.

For eyebrow transplantation, approximately 500–1000 grafts are usually sufficient. During this procedure, hair follicles are taken from the occipital area using the FUE method and transplanted into the eyebrow area. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia.

Eyebrow transplantation is performed using the FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) or DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) method. Hair grafts are collected from the occiput using the FUE method and transplanted into the eyebrow area using implantation technique.

To ensure that the patient does not feel pain during the procedure, local anesthesia is applied to the eyebrow area and the donor zone. Thus, the sensation of pain during the procedure is completely excluded.

After eyebrow transplantation, all or part of the eyebrows may fall out after some time. However, the fallen hairs usually regrow within 3 months.
