How the Surgery Works

Stages of Beard Transplant

01
Consultation and Diagnosis
The condition of the facial skin is assessed, as well as the structure of the beard and donor area. The number of required grafts is determined and the appropriate method is selected — DHI or FUE Sapphire.
02
Beard Line Planning
Together with the patient, the desired shape and density of the beard are determined. The line is drawn taking into account the face shape and individual preferences.
03
Anesthesia and Preparation
The beard area and donor zone are numbed with local anesthesia. You will not feel pain throughout the entire procedure.
04
Donor Graft Extraction
Hair follicles are taken from the occipital area using micromotors. Single and double grafts are carefully extracted without leaving visible traces.
05
Graft Implantation
Grafts are implanted into the beard area at the correct angle. It is very important to place the hair transplants at an angle matching the natural growth direction.
06
Instructions and Aftercare
After the operation, the patient receives detailed care instructions. The area can be washed the next day. Within 1–2 weeks, the first signs of growth become visible.

Everything About Beard Transplant

As with hair transplantation using the FUE and DHI methods, beard transplantation involves collecting single and double hair follicles from the area between the ears (the donor zone) using micromotors, and transplanting them using the DHI or Sapphire FUE method. There is no difference in application compared to hair transplantation. During beard transplantation, you will not experience pain.
The number of grafts used in beard transplantation may vary depending on the patient's beard density. However, if the beard transplant is performed only in the mustache area, 500–1000 grafts are sufficient. Single and double hair grafts are taken from the area between the ears, i.e., the donor zone, using micromotors.
Today, beard transplantation can also be applied for baldness, burns, and scars. Depending on the patient's condition, our specialists determine what procedures need to be performed before the beard transplant. The beard transplant is performed under fully sterile conditions in our clinic by our specialists using the latest technological methods.
After beard transplantation, redness and a small amount of blood may be observed on the transplanted area for 6–8 days, but this is temporary. After the transplant, a crust forms, and in the first week, special washing is required. Thanks to the DHI Choi and Sapphire FUE methods used during beard transplantation, the operation is performed without scars.
After beard transplantation, crusts and mild itching may appear on the transplant site. These symptoms may persist for 1–2 weeks. The transplanted grafts are expected to shed within the first month. Between 3 and 6 months, the beard grows back and finally takes its shape.

For faster and healthier beard growth after transplantation, specialists recommend massaging the transplanted area, consuming nutritious foods, and maintaining a stress-free lifestyle.
  • In the first days after the transplant, slight swelling and redness may be observed — this is a normal reaction.
  • During the first week, crust formation and its shedding may occur — this is also a normal process.
  • After the first week, the area gradually begins to heal. Most grafts grow within 2–3 weeks.
  • After the first month, the area is completely healed, and most of the hair has attached.
  • To speed up the healing process, it is necessary to follow the doctor's recommendations: maintain cleanliness, use creams, and protect from direct sunlight.
  • You can trim your beard just 2 weeks after the transplant.
