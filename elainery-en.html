About the treatment

What are
aligners?

Aligners are clear, removable trays made of medical-grade plastic that gradually move your teeth into the right position. Unlike braces, they're virtually invisible in everyday life.

Every set is custom-made from a digital 3D scan of your teeth. You'll see how your smile will look in 6, 12, or 18 months — even before treatment begins.

Indications

Which cases are
suitable for aligners?

Aligners are effective for most bite and tooth-position issues:

Crooked & crowded teeth

Aligners gradually straighten teeth, fixing crowding and misalignment — no painful braces needed.

Gaps between teeth

Diastemas and gaps are closed in a few months — no surgery required.

Bite problems

Deep, open, or cross bite — aligners correct the position of both jaws and normalize how teeth meet.

Pre-prosthetic prep

Before veneers, crowns, or implants, aligners help position your teeth for the best result.

Relapse after braces

Teeth shifted again after braces? Aligners gently bring them back into place — no need for a full retreatment.

Discreet treatment

The perfect choice for adult patients who want straighter teeth without drawing attention to their smile during treatment.

Treatment steps

How aligner treatment
works at our clinic

The whole process is well organized — from your first consultation to the final result.

01
Consultation & 3D scan

The orthodontist examines your teeth, takes a 3D digital scan, and evaluates your bite. A preliminary plan is built with a result preview.

02
Plan approval & aligner order

You see the final result on screen and approve the plan. Your custom set of aligners for the entire course is then manufactured.

03
Receiving your aligners

You receive the full set of aligners with instructions. Switch them yourself every 1–2 weeks. Check-up visits are every 2–3 months.

04
Final result

Once your course is complete, a retainer is made to lock in the result. Your teeth stay securely in their new position.

How to wear and care for your aligners

Taking care of your aligners is key to comfort and a fast result. Follow these simple rules:

  • Wear the aligners for at least 20–22 hours a day
  • Remove only when eating and brushing
  • Rinse with cool water (never hot!)
  • Clean with a soft brush — no toothpaste
  • Store in the provided case when removed
