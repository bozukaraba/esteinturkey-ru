FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the hair transplant hurt?
The procedure is completely painless and comfortable thanks to local anaesthesia. However, the actual injection of the anaesthetic can be slightly uncomfortable, but the sensation only lasts a few seconds.
How many grafts are transplanted on average?
Usually between 2,500 and 5,000 grafts — depending on the area of baldness, the donor zone, and the desired density.
Do I need to shave my head?
The DHI method does not require a full shave: we only shave the donor area. With FUE, a full head shave is always required.
How much does a hair transplant in Turkey cost?
We tailor the solution to each patient individually. The exact price depends on the chosen method and the number of grafts, and is determined after an online consultation. Contact us to find out the price for your specific case.
Is there a guarantee on the result?
Yes, we provide an official guarantee certificate confirming graft survival and the reliability of the result. We also support the patient after the operation to ensure a smooth recovery.
