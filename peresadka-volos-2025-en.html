Why a transplant

Why people get a hair transplant

Hair loss affects confidence, appearance, and everyday life. A transplant offers a long-term, natural solution.

01

Genetic hair loss

Hereditary hair loss is the most common cause of thinning and baldness. A transplant restores natural hair where it matters most.

02

Age-related changes

As we age, hair growth slows and strands become finer. A transplant helps restore a younger, fuller look.

03

Loss of confidence

Thinning hair affects self-esteem. Restoring your hairline boosts confidence and quality of life.

04

Hormonal causes

Hormonal changes, illness, or stress can trigger hair loss. Transplantation offers a lasting solution.

FAQ

Answers to your questions

Everything you need to know before a hair transplant.

Is the transplant painful?
The procedure is painless thanks to local anaesthesia. The moment of injection may feel a little sensitive, but it only lasts a few seconds.
How many grafts are transplanted on average?
Typically between 2,500 and 5,000 grafts — depending on the bald area, donor zone, and desired density.
Do I need to shave my head?
DHI does not require a full shave — only the donor area is trimmed. FUE usually requires shaving the whole head.
How much does a hair transplant cost in Turkey?
The solution is tailored to you. The exact cost depends on the method and the number of grafts and is determined after an online consultation. Contact us to learn your personalised price.
Is there a guarantee on the result?
Yes — we provide an official guarantee certificate and follow up with every patient for a smooth recovery.
