When to see us

When do you need
dental treatment?

Don't postpone your visit — many issues are easy to fix when caught early:

Tooth pain and sensitivity

Sharp or aching pain, and sensitivity to hot, cold, or sweet — the first signs of cavities or pulp inflammation.

Visible damage and dark spots

Dark spots, rough patches, or visible cavities on the surface of your teeth indicate the development of decay.

Bleeding gums

Bleeding or inflamed gums are a symptom of gingivitis or periodontitis and require timely treatment.

Old or lost fillings

Lost, cracked, or aged fillings need to be replaced to prevent further decay and discomfort.

Preventive check-up

Regular check-ups 1–2 times a year help catch issues early and avoid costly treatment later.

Pre-prosthetic prep

Before crowns, veneers, or implants, all inflammation and decay in the mouth must be treated.

Types of treatment

Which services are available
at Este in Turkey?

We provide all types of general dental treatment in a single clinic.

Most popular
Cavity treatment

Removal of decayed tissue and placement of a modern light-cured filling. The treatment is done in a single visit, painless under anesthesia. Fillings are color-matched to your teeth.

Complex treatment
Root canal (endodontics)

Treatment of pulpitis and periodontitis — removal of the inflamed nerve, cleaning and sealing the root canals. Performed under a microscope for maximum precision.

Aesthetics
Aesthetic restoration

Restoration of shape, color, and function of damaged teeth with next-generation composite materials. The result looks like a natural tooth.

Prevention
Gum treatment (periodontics)

Treatment of gingivitis and periodontitis — professional cleaning, scaling, and curettage. Healthy gums are the foundation of any lasting result.

Hygiene
Professional cleaning

Removal of plaque and tartar with ultrasound and Air Flow. Polishing and fluoride application. Recommended every 6 months.

Diagnostics
3D tomography & X-ray

Digital X-ray and cone-beam CT (CBCT) give an accurate picture of your teeth, roots, and bone before any treatment.
