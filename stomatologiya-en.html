FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about dental treatment in Turkey.

Is it safe to get dental treatment in Turkey?
Yes. Turkish dental clinics hold international JCI and ISO certifications. We use the same materials and technologies as leading European clinics. Our surgeons have trained in Europe and the USA.
How many days do I need to stay in Istanbul?
It depends on the treatment. Veneers and crowns — 5–7 days. Implants — 3–5 days (with temporary crowns), then return after 3–6 months for permanent ones. All-on-4 — 5–7 days.
How much cheaper is Turkey than Europe?
On average 3–5 times. For example, one implant with a crown in Europe can cost €1,500–€2,500; with us it starts from €700 with the same quality materials. Even with flights and hotel, the savings are significant.
What if I have problems after returning home?
We provide online support. We consult remotely and, if necessary, refer you to partner clinics in your city. For warranty cases, we organize a follow-up visit at our expense.
How long do veneers and implants last?
Porcelain veneers last 15–20 years with proper care. Zirconia ones — 20–25 years. Implants — a lifetime with healthy gums. We provide a written warranty for all restorations.
