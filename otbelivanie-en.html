Indications

Who is
whitening for?

Professional whitening is effective in most cases of tooth discoloration. Here is when it is especially useful:

Coffee & tea discoloration

Regular consumption of coffee, tea, red wine and other staining beverages gradually colors enamel. Professional whitening removes these stains.

Smoking stains

Nicotine and tar leave persistent yellow and brown stains. Flash and Opalescence effectively lighten even this discoloration.

Age-related discoloration

As we age, enamel thins and teeth become darker. Whitening restores brightness and freshness of your smile without aggressive intervention.

Preparation for an event

Wedding, graduation, photo shoot or important meeting — Flash whitening in 45 minutes will help you look impeccable.

Desire for a beautiful smile

Just want to smile more confidently? Professional whitening is the quickest and safest way to refresh the appearance of your teeth.

After orthodontic treatment

After braces removal, whitening restores color and gives teeth an even, glowing shade.

Procedure steps

How does whitening work?

The entire process takes just one visit — from diagnosis to the final result.

01
Consultation & examination

The doctor assesses the enamel condition, determines the type of discoloration and selects the optimal method — Flash or Opalescence.

02
Professional cleaning

Before whitening, dental plaque and calculus are removed — this ensures even and effective gel application.

03
Gel application & activation

Gums are protected with a special barrier, then the whitening gel is applied. In the Flash method, it is activated with a UV lamp.

04
Remineralization & assessment

After the procedure, the enamel is strengthened with a remineralizing compound. The doctor evaluates the result and gives care recommendations.

After-whitening care

Follow simple rules so the result lasts as long as possible.

  • Avoid staining foods and beverages for the first 48 hours
  • Do not smoke for 24–48 hours after the procedure
  • Use toothpaste for sensitive teeth
  • Regularly visit the dentist for preventive cleaning
