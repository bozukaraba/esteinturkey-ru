About the procedure

What are
veneers?

Veneers are thin shells made of durable ceramic or zirconia that are placed on the front surface of teeth. They are ideal for correcting the shape, color and aesthetics of your smile.

We use only premium materials. All are hypoallergenic and look like natural teeth.

Indications

When are
veneers needed?

Veneers are a universal solution for most aesthetic dental problems. Here is when they are especially effective:

Crooked or chipped teeth

Veneers mask chips, cracks, shape irregularities and align the smile line without braces.

Discoloration and stains

Persistent stains from coffee, tea, tetracycline or fluorosis that cannot be removed by whitening.

Large gaps

Diastemas and gaps between teeth are eliminated without lengthy orthodontic treatment.

"Hollywood Smile

The desire to get perfectly straight, snow-white teeth with an ideal shape — that is exactly what veneers are created for.

Restoration after treatment

Restoring aesthetics after caries treatment, root canal therapy or other dental procedures.

Treatment stages

How is veneer placement done?

In most cases the entire process takes just 3–5 days.

01
Consultation & 3D scanning

The doctor performs an examination, takes a 3D tomogram and digital modeling — you see your future smile even before the procedure.

02
Tooth preparation (minimal)

If necessary, light grinding of the tooth surface is performed. For ultra-thin veneers, grinding is often not needed at all.

03
Lab fabrication

Impressions or digital data are sent to the dental lab. Technicians fabricate veneers precisely to the shape of your teeth.

04
Fitting & bite check

Veneers are tried on, adjusted if necessary and fixed with special dental cement. The bite is checked.

How to care for veneers?

Simple care will ensure your veneers last as long as possible — up to 20 years.

  • Brush teeth twice a day — morning and evening
  • Use a soft brush and non-fluoride toothpaste
  • Avoid excessively hard or sticky food
  • Visit the dentist once a year for check-ups
