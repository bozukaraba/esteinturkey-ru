About the Method

What is DHI Hair Transplantation?

The DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) method is the most advanced hair transplant technique, where a special Choi implanter pen allows the surgeon to open the channel and implant the hair follicle in a single step.

Unlike FUE Sapphire, DHI does not require prior channel opening. The Choi pen is equipped with a hollow needle with a diameter of 0.6–1 mm. A graft is loaded into it, and with a single press, the follicle is placed into the skin at the precise angle and depth.

It is this principle that gives DHI unmatched precision in growth direction and makes it the method of choice when density needs to be increased in areas where hair is still present.

Why DHI?

Three Key Advantages of the Choi Pen

Choi Implanter Pen: channel and implantation in one step
The cylindrical hollow needle of the Choi pen simultaneously punctures the skin and plants the follicle. This minimizes the time the graft spends outside the body, improving survival and engraftment quality.
Full control of angle, direction, and implantation depth
The pen design lets the surgeon precisely set the tilt angle and direction for each graft. The result: hair grows exactly where it should, fully mirroring the natural growth pattern.
Unshaved DHI — no shaving, no visible traces
DHI can be performed without fully shaving the head. This is the ideal option for those who want to keep the procedure completely confidential — no one will notice.

How It Happens

Stages of the DHI Choi Operation

The surgery takes 7–9 hours and is performed under local anesthesia. Choi pens of different diameters are selected individually to match the size of your grafts.

1
Preliminary Consultation and Diagnosis
The trichologist evaluates the type and stage of hair loss, reviews your medical history, performs dermatoscopy of the donor area, and determines the optimal method.
2
Hairline Design and Marking
Together with the patient, the surgeon designs a natural hairline, taking into account facial proportions and the long-term picture of possible future hair loss.
3
Local Anesthesia of the Donor Area
The occipital and lateral zones are numbed with local anesthesia. If desired, needle-free anesthesia is used for maximum comfort.
4
Graft Extraction with Micromotor
Grafts are individually extracted from the donor zone using a micropunch of 0.6–0.9 mm diameter — no incisions, no scars. High-quality follicles with 2–3 hairs.
5
Classification and Loading into Choi Pens
Grafts are sorted by hair count. Each follicle is loaded into the hollow needle of the appropriately sized Choi pen — immediately before implantation.
6
Anesthesia of the Recipient Area
The transplant zone is locally anesthetized. If needed, a tumescent solution is applied to reduce swelling and protect the tissues.
7
Direct Implantation with Choi Pen
With a press of the plunger, the surgeon simultaneously opens the microchannel and plants the graft at the set angle. This stage takes about 4 hours and requires the highest level of team expertise.
8
Dressing and Post-operative Instructions
The donor area is bandaged. The next day, the first wash is done at the clinic. Antibiotics and care products are prescribed. Return to normal life within 6–8 days.

How Does DHI Choi Hair Transplant Work?

Before the procedure, the patient's overall health condition is analyzed. Blood tests, a pre-operative consultation, and a questionnaire are conducted. The transplant area and new hairline are planned according to the patient's wishes and expectations.

  • DHI Choi hair transplant can be performed without fully shaving the head — only the donor area is trimmed.
  • The surgery is performed under local anesthesia or, upon request, with sedation.
  • Hair follicles are extracted one by one from the donor zone using homogeneous intersection with a micromotor — the same way as in the FUE method.
  • Grafts are carefully loaded into a Choi implanter with a diameter of 0.6 to 1 mm and implanted directly into the skin, bypassing the prior channel-opening stage.
  • Thanks to the DHI Choi Pen, the implantation result looks natural and dense.
  • The Choi implanter allows individual adjustment of the angle, direction, and depth of each graft to achieve the most natural and aesthetic result.
  • Transplanted hair continues to grow for life and does not fall out in the absence of significant health deterioration.
  • All procedures are performed by specialists holding European certificates who have completed dedicated DHI Choi hair transplant training.
