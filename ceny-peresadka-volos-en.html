Factors that affect the cost of a hair transplant

The price is built individually and depends on a number of parameters:

1. Hair transplant method

  • FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) — the most popular technique. Hairs are transplanted one by one, which rules out scars. FUE is more affordable than DHI.
  • DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) — a more modern method where grafts are implanted directly into the skin with a special Choi Pen. The procedure is more expensive but achieves high density and a faster recovery.

2. Number of grafts

Hair follicles are always calculated by grafts (follicular units) that need to be transplanted. Typically:

  • up to 2,000 grafts — suitable for refining receding areas
  • 2,500–3,500 grafts — restoring the hairline and crown area
  • 4,000–5,000 grafts — full coverage of large areas

3. Experience of the clinic and surgeon

There are dozens of clinics in Istanbul, but not all deliver the same level of quality. The team's experience, international certifications and patient reviews directly influence the price.

4. Included services

Usually the cost of a hair transplant in Turkey covers not just the procedure but a full package:

  • airport transfer both ways
  • hotel accommodation
  • pre-operative tests
  • consultations
  • medication, shampoos, lotions
  • post-operative care

So the cost of a hair transplant is not just the procedure — it is a complete end-to-end patient experience.

Average cost of a hair transplant in Turkey and Istanbul

Compared with global prices, Turkey remains the leader in affordability:

  • ✅FUE hair transplant: from €1,500–2,000 per session.
  • ✅DHI hair transplant: from €2,000–2,500.
For comparison: in Europe and the US the same procedure costs from €5,000 to €10,000.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Does the hair transplant hurt?
The procedure is completely painless and comfortable thanks to local anaesthesia. However, the actual injection of the anaesthetic can be slightly uncomfortable, but the sensation lasts only a few seconds.
How many grafts are transplanted on average?
Usually between 2,500 and 5,000 grafts — depending on the area of baldness, the donor zone, and the desired density.
Do I need to shave my head?
The DHI method does not require a full shave: we only shave the donor area. With FUE, a full head shave is always required.
