About the procedure

Dental implants —
a reliable solution for life

Dental implants are one of the most reliable and aesthetically pleasing ways to restore missing teeth. Modern dental implants fully mimic the natural root and deliver exceptional comfort, durability, and a natural appearance.

This approach is suitable for nearly every patient and allows you to regain full chewing function in a short time after the procedure. An implant is an artificial tooth root made of titanium — a safe, allergy-free material that integrates with the bone exceptionally well.

By choosing our clinic for your implants, you'll get a result that looks and feels just like your own teeth — natural, reliable, and built to last.

Types of implants

Which implant options are available at ESTE IN TURKEY?

01
Classic implants
The standard method: one implant replaces one missing tooth. Ideal for single or multiple missing teeth. Placement: 1–2 visits; osseointegration: 3–6 months.
1–2 teeth 3–6 mo. Titanium
02
All-on-4 / All-on-6
A full-arch restoration supported by 4 or 6 implants. A complete set of teeth is fixed together with an artificial gum. Hybrid materials include acrylic, zirconia, or metal-ceramic. Ideal for fully edentulous patients.
Full arch 4–6 implants Zirconia
03
Subperiosteal implants
Used when the jawbone is too thin or atrophied. The implant rests on top of the bone, beneath the periosteum — a safe option when other methods are not feasible.

Treatment steps

How the dental implant process works

01
Online consultation
Send us your panoramic X-ray or CT scan. The doctor reviews your case and creates a preliminary treatment plan with cost estimate.
02
3D diagnostics & plan
At the clinic we perform 3D tomography. Based on it, we draft a detailed plan: number of implants, types, timeline, and materials.
03
Implant placement
The surgeon places the titanium posts into the bone under local anesthesia. Temporary crowns or healing caps are attached afterwards.
04
Osseointegration
Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with the bone. You stay at home during this period — our doctor is available for online follow-up.
05
Second visit: abutment
We place the abutment — the connector between the implant and the crown. Impressions are taken to fabricate the final crown.
06
Final crown
The permanent crown — zirconia or metal-ceramic — is fitted. Treatment is complete: you head home with a brand-new smile.

Frequently asked

Questions about dental implants

An implant is an artificial tooth root made of titanium. This material is safe, allergy-free, and integrates with the bone exceptionally well. On top of the implant, an abutment and a crown are placed, mimicking a natural tooth in shape and shade.
Implants are recommended when: one or several teeth are missing; a full arch needs to be restored; removable dentures are uncomfortable; there is bone loss (a basal or subperiosteal solution may be an option). Contraindications include severe inflammation, uncontrolled diabetes, cancer, and some systemic conditions.
Implant placement itself takes from one day to a few days, followed by 6 days of in-clinic observation. Temporary crowns are placed if needed. After 3–6 months, the permanent crowns are fitted. In total, treatment requires 2 visits to Istanbul — 3 in rare cases.
This method is designed for fully edentulous patients. A full arch of teeth — together with an artificial gum — is fixed on 4 or 6 implants. The All-on protocol uses a hybrid material: acrylic, zirconia, or metal-ceramic. It is the most cost-effective and fastest way to restore a complete set of teeth.
We work exclusively with original brands: MediGMA, Iris, Megagen, Osstem, Nobel, Straumann, Venus and other Europe-certified systems. Every implant comes with a warranty and a passport with its serial number.
You receive: the official manufacturer warranty on the implants; a clinic warranty on the doctor's work; and an implant passport with serial number and manufacturer details.
