FAQ

Frequently asked questions about hair transplant in Turkey

Answers to the most common questions about hair transplant in Turkey.

Is a hair transplant painful?
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. You may feel slight pressure, but not pain. After the procedure there can be mild discomfort for 1–2 days, which is managed with painkillers.
When will I see the results?
The first new hairs appear after 3–4 months. By 6 months roughly 60–70% of growth is visible. The full result is seen 10–12 months after the procedure.
How many days should I stay in Istanbul?
You need at least 2–3 days: arrival day, surgery day, and a follow-up check the next day. We recommend staying 3–4 days to rest a bit in Istanbul.
Will I have enough donor hair?
This is determined during consultation. Our specialists evaluate the donor area and calculate how many grafts can be safely extracted. Even with a limited donor supply we maximize the result.
Will the transplanted hair fall out again?
The transplanted hair is taken from an area that is genetically resistant to hair loss. After the grafts take (3–6 months) they stay forever. That is exactly why we offer a lifetime guarantee.

Why choose a hair transplant in Turkey?

A hair transplant in Turkey offers the best balance of price and quality. Turkish clinics are equipped with the latest technology and our doctors hold international certifications. FUE Sapphire and DHI-Choi methods deliver a natural result with minimal discomfort.
